Investigative Confidentiality & Client Data Protection

Compitiello & Donofrio Private Investigations (CAD Private Investigations DBA I-9 Insights) operates under supervisory federal law enforcement standards. Every inquiry and case is treated with absolute integrity, surgical precision, and total privacy.

Confidentiality is the core foundation of our investigative work. We do not sell, lease, or rent client information to third parties.
