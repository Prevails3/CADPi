FLORIDA DEBTOR ENCLAVES

Piercing the Sunshine State's Asset Protection Labyrinth

Florida is widely recognized by judgment creditors, litigators, and forensic investigators as one of the most challenging asset recovery jurisdictions in the nation. With virtually unlimited constitutional homestead protections (Florida Constitution Article X, Section 4), statutory tenancy by the entireties exemptions, and close proximity to offshore Caribbean banking havens (Bahamas, Cayman Islands, Nevis), dishonest debtors and wealthy spouses frequently relocate capital to Florida to defeat lawful collection.

Compitiello & Donofrio Private Investigations brings federal-grade investigative methodology to Florida asset recovery. Our leadership spent decades directing federal kleptocracy, customs fraud, and financial task forces that specialized in tracking international money laundering pipelines that terminate in South Florida real estate, luxury marine vessels, and pass-through LLCs.

"Debtors believe Florida's homestead laws make them untouchable. When capital was fraudulently converted from out-of-state entities, federal forensic tracing provides the exact paper trail required to pierce exemptions."
