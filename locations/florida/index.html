Florida Statutory Context for Private Investigations

Audio Surveillance (Two-Party (All-Party) Consent): Florida is an all-party consent jurisdiction under Fla. Stat. § 934.03. Intercepting or recording oral or wire communications without the consent of all parties is a third-degree felony and strictly inadmissible in Florida state and administrative proceedings.

Electronic Tracking (Fla. Stat. § 934.425): Under Fla. Stat. § 934.425, placing a tracking device or tracking application on a motor vehicle without the consent of all owners or authorized lessees is a second-degree misdemeanor, with narrow business fleet owner exemptions.
