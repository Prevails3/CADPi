New York Statutory Context for Private Investigations

Audio Surveillance (One-Party Consent): New York is a one-party consent state under N.Y. Penal Law §§ 250.00 and 250.05. It is legal to record an in-person conversation or telephone call as long as at least one party to the communication consents. Unlawfully intercepting communications without any party's consent is a Class E felony under New York law.

Electronic Tracking (N.Y. Penal Law § 120.45(4) (Stalking in the Fourth Degree)): Under N.Y. Penal Law § 120.45(4), installing an unauthorized GPS device or electronic tracking beacon without the vehicle owner's consent constitutes criminal stalking and results in complete evidentiary suppression in New York Supreme Court proceedings.
