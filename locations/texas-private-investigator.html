TEXAS COMMERCIAL DYNAMICS

Forensic Intelligence Across the Lone Star State

Texas represents one of the nation's most vibrant economic engines, driven by commercial finance in Dallas, energy and international shipping in Houston, tech entrepreneurship in Austin, and major aerospace corridors. Due to strong statutory asset exemptions and rapid commercial entity formation, commercial litigators and judgment creditors frequently encounter complex corporate veils and multi-layered partnerships designed to frustrate asset recovery.

Compitiello & Donofrio Private Investigations serves corporate legal departments, commercial litigation boutiques, and high-net-worth individuals involved in Texas disputes. Led by former federal supervisory special agents with decades of experience unmasking white-collar fraud schemes, we pierce nominee corporate shells, locate commercial bank accounts for writ of garnishment, and support post-judgment turnover orders.

"Texas debtors frequently assume broad statutory protections shield their commercial ventures. We follow corporate cash flows, customer receivables, and alter-ego entities to reveal unencumbered recovery targets."
