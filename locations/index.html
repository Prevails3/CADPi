Frequently Asked Questions Regarding Nationwide Operations

How does CAD Private Investigations coordinate multi-state investigations?

All multi-state and nationwide cases are centrally directed from our Corporate Headquarters in Scottsdale, Arizona by former federal supervisory special agents. Field operations are executed in direct alignment with state-licensed investigators and nationwide task force reciprocity channels.

Can CAD trace assets moved across state lines?

Yes. One of our core specialties is unmasking interstate and cross-border asset concealment, such as funds transferred from California, New York, or Texas into Florida real estate, Delaware holding companies, or Wyoming privacy trusts.

Do you handle foreign and international investigations?

Yes. Our leadership directed international kleptocracy and financial crime task forces at DHS/HSI, coordinating complex foreign asset seizures, offshore bank inquiries, and cross-border witness location.
