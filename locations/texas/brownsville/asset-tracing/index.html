Texas Statutory Context

Wiretap & Recording Compliance (One-Party Consent): Texas is a one-party consent jurisdiction under Tex. Penal Code § 16.02. A person can record a wire, oral, or electronic communication to which they are a party, or where one of the parties has given prior consent. Recording without at least one party's consent is a state jail felony.

Tracking Constraints (Tex. Penal Code § 16.06): Under Tex. Penal Code § 16.06 (Unlawful Installation of Tracking Device), it is a Class A misdemeanor to install an electronic tracking device on a motor vehicle owned or leased by another without their effective consent. Evidence collected through unauthorized tracking is strictly barred in Texas courts.
