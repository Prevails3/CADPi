CALIFORNIA INTERSTATE DYNAMICS

Navigating Cross-Border Asset Concealment & Corporate Disputes

California represents the world's fifth-largest economy and the single largest corridor of domestic capital migration into neighboring states like Arizona, Nevada, and Texas. When wealthy spouses, commercial debtors, or fraudulent business partners face litigation in California courts, their first instinct is frequently to siphon liquidity across state borders—establishing pass-through LLCs in Wyoming or Delaware, acquiring luxury real estate in Scottsdale or Las Vegas, and sheltering capital away from California discovery.

Compitiello & Donofrio Private Investigations serves California attorneys, venture funds, family offices, and private principals. Operating with former federal supervisory authority, we pierce nominee entity veils, follow multi-state electronic banking trails, and deliver evidence admissible under both the California Evidence Code and Federal Rules of Civil Procedure.

"Debtors and litigious spouses in Los Angeles and the Bay Area rarely keep hidden funds within California borders. We track capital the moment it leaves California jurisdiction."
