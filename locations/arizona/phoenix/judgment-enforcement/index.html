Arizona Statutory Context

Wiretap & Recording Compliance (One-Party Consent): Under Arizona law (A.R.S. § 13-3005), Arizona is a one-party consent state. An investigator or operative may record in-person or wire communications provided at least one party to the conversation has consented. Surreptitiously recording without any party's consent is a Class 6 felony.

Tracking Constraints (A.R.S. § 13-2923 (Stalking) & A.R.S. § 13-3019 (Surveillance)): Under Arizona Revised Statutes, installing an electronic tracking or GPS device is lawful only when authorized with the effective consent of the registered vehicle owner or authorized corporate custodian. Tracking without owner consent violates Arizona anti-stalking laws and renders evidence inadmissible.

Frequently Asked Questions Regarding Judgment Recovery in Phoenix, Arizona

How do you enforce an unpaid judgment in Phoenix and Maricopa County courts?

We conduct deep post-judgment debtor asset discovery to identify unencumbered bank accounts, commercial receivables, real estate equity, and business revenue streams, providing local counsel with exact targets for writs of garnishment and execution.

What if a debtor in Phoenix transferred assets to family members or shell companies?

We investigate fraudulent conveyances under the Uniform Voidable Transactions Act (UVTA / TUFTA). By establishing 'badges of fraud'—such as transfers for less than fair value while insolvent—we enable attorneys to void the transfers and claw assets back into the judgment debtor's estate.

Can out-of-state judgments be enforced against debtors residing in Phoenix, Arizona?

Yes. We support sister-state judgment domestication under the Uniform Enforcement of Foreign Judgments Act (UEFJA), verifying debtor residency and locating local assets before filing for domestication in Maricopa County Superior Court - Central Court Building.

What attachable assets can CAD identify for post-judgment garnishment?

We identify operating bank accounts, payroll distributions, commercial client receivables, heavy machinery, registered transport fleets, and unencumbered commercial real estate suitable for judicial sale or charging orders.
