DIGITAL EVIDENCE PROTOCOLS

Why Proper Forensic Handling Wins Injunctions

When an executive or technical specialist resigns to join a competitor, internal corporate IT often attempts to inspect the departed employee's laptop. Powering on the machine or casually browsing folders modifies hundreds of system timestamps, opening the door for opposing counsel to allege evidence spoliation.

Our computer forensic specialists, Alex Nader & Scott Unger, preserve pristine bit-stream forensic images with SHA-256 cryptographic hashes. We establish irrefutable evidence timelines showing exactly which files were copied, what USB drives were connected, and which cloud accounts received proprietary data.

"Departing employees believe that deleting browsing history or emptying the recycle bin erases their digital footprints. In modern operating systems, every file movement leaves an indelible forensic record."
