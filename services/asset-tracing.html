Why Standard Discovery Fails to Find Hidden Assets

In high-net-worth divorce proceedings, commercial partnership dissolutions, and post-judgment enforcement, opponents rarely keep hidden capital in obvious domestic savings accounts. Standard civil subpoenas and interrogatories only produce what opposing counsel already knows to ask for. Sophisticated parties deliberately obscure ownership through shell corporations, nominee trust structures, and cross-border entities.

Led by former DHS/HSI Supervisory Special Agent Michael Compitiello—who oversaw international money laundering task forces, kleptocracy asset seizures, and multi-target fraud rings—Compitiello & Donofrio Private Investigations approaches financial intelligence from the outside in. We follow verifiable digital and statutory paper trails that subpoenas cannot uncover alone.

"Asset concealment is not magic; it is an architectural puzzle built on shell entities, nominee agreements, and banking friction. Given methodical forensic tracing, every layer can be peeled back."

Four Primary Concealment Channels We Uncover

  • Domestic Secrecy LLCs: Entities formed in Wyoming, Delaware, or Nevada where beneficial ownership is obscured from public secretary of state portals.
  • Offshore Asset Protection Trusts: Cross-border entities in Nevis, the Cook Islands, or Belize drafted with flee clauses to defeat creditors.
  • Collusive Liens & Straw Mortgages: Fictitious loans placed against prime real estate to make multimillion-dollar equity appear completely underwater.
  • Digital Currency & Unhosted Wallets: Layering liquid capital through cold storage hardware wallets and decentralized protocols.
THE C&D DIFFERENCE

Our 3-Phase Federal Asset Tracing Framework

We apply the exact investigative protocols utilized in federal criminal financial prosecutions, translating raw records into clear, undeniable court exhibits:

Phase 1: Source-and-Application Lifestyle Audit

We establish a mathematical baseline comparing declared income against verifiable lifestyle expenditures—such as luxury vehicle leases, private aviation, country club memberships, and real property upkeep. When outlays exceed declared revenues by hundreds of thousands of dollars, Arizona judges routinely infer undisclosed income streams.

Phase 2: Piercing Nominee & Corporate Veils

By cross-analyzing UCC financing statements, historical registered agent addresses, corporate signature blocks, and corporate domain registries, we identify recurring straw managers and link the subject directly to concealed assets.

Phase 3: SWIFT Wire & Correspondent Banking Tracing

Because international funds must clear through domestic correspondent accounts, recurring round-dollar wire transfers reveal offshore accounts and foreign depository entities.

COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a private investigator find hidden bank accounts in a divorce?

Yes. Private investigators employ lawful, federal-grade investigative methodologies—such as lifestyle audits, public records synthesis, UCC filing examinations, and corporate registries—to locate institutions and cash flows without violating the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA).

How do you uncover assets hidden in Wyoming, Delaware, or Nevada LLCs?

While statutory privacy states do not list beneficial owners on their online registries, we identify cross-references across asset purchase deeds, commercial lender disclosures, historical filings, domain ownership records, and shared registered agent networks to pierce the corporate veil.

Will the evidence be admissible in Arizona Family & Superior Courts?

Yes. Every investigative report compiled by CAD Private Investigations adheres strictly to Arizona Rules of Evidence and federal chain-of-custody protocols. Our team is prepared to provide sworn affidavits and expert witness testimony under Rule 702.
