COMMERCIAL JUDGMENT ENFORCEMENT

Turning Uncollectible Judgments Into Enforceable Levies

Securing a multi-million-dollar civil judgment, arbitration award, or commercial verdict is frequently only half the battle. Experienced judgment debtors rarely wait for a sheriff's levy; they proactively shift capital into pass-through entities, encumber properties with straw mortgages, transfer titles to family members, and disperse funds across multiple banking networks.

Standard post-judgment debtor examinations (judgment debtor interrogatories) are often ineffective against dishonest debtors willing to conceal assets under oath. Compitiello & Donofrio Private Investigations provides judgment creditors and commercial litigators with an aggressive, intelligence-led asset discovery operation designed to find tangible, unencumbered targets for writ of garnishment and execution.

"A judgment is merely a piece of paper until you locate the specific accounts, properties, and receivables to execute against. We identify the exact physical and institutional targets needed to compel satisfaction."

Key Asset Classes We Locate for Judgment Creditors

  • Commercial & Operating Bank Accounts: Active depository relationships and merchant processing accounts to support immediate writs of garnishment.
  • Unencumbered Real Estate & Equity Spreads: Title research identifying properties where actual market value substantially exceeds recorded liens.
  • Corporate Receivables & Customer Invoices: Third-party entities owing money to the debtor that can be garnished directly through third-party writs.
  • Heavy Machinery, Aircraft & Fleet Vehicles: FAA aircraft registries, DOT commercial vehicle databases, and equipment UCC lien records.
  • Distributions from Nominee LLCs: Pass-through capital flow and profit distributions from corporate shells where the debtor exercises alter-ego control.
  • Out-of-State Property Across Multi-Jurisdiction Corridors: Assets relocated from California, New York, or Illinois into Arizona, Nevada, or Texas enclaves.

Our 4-Phase Creditor Asset Recovery Protocol

Led by former federal supervisory special agents who spent careers dismantling transnational financial fraud, our team executes a structured post-judgment playbook:

Phase 1: Comprehensive Entity & Affiliation Deconstruction

We unspool the debtor's entire corporate footprint—identifying active LLCs, corporate partnerships, assumed business names (DBAs), and family trusts across all 50 state secretary of state registries.

Phase 2: Fraudulent Conveyance & 'Badges of Fraud' Audit

We analyze real property deed transfers, LLC ownership amendments, and recorded liens executed shortly before or during the underlying litigation. Proving 'badges of fraud' under the Uniform Voidable Transactions Act (UVTA) enables your legal counsel to void sham conveyances.

Phase 3: Banking Network & Revenue Stream Identification

Through lawful open-source financial telemetry, commercial disclosures, UCC filings, and transaction records, we map where the debtor conducts regular banking operations, giving your counsel exact targets for bank levies.

Phase 4: Actionable Levy Dossier & Deposition Cross-Examination Support

We provide an actionable execution dossier with legal property descriptions, garnishment target addresses, and tailored debtor-examination questioning outlines to expose perjury in court.

INTERSTATE DOMESTICATION

Domesticating Foreign Judgments in Arizona (UEFJA)

Did you secure a commercial judgment in California, Nevada, New York, Texas, or Illinois against an entity that relocated operations to Phoenix, Scottsdale, or the broader Southwest? Under the Uniform Enforcement of Foreign Judgments Act (A.R.S. § 12-1701 et seq.), foreign sister-state judgments can be registered and enforced in Arizona with the full weight of an Arizona Superior Court order.

CAD Private Investigations helps out-of-state law firms pinpoint exact Arizona-sited assets—real estate in Maricopa County, corporate bank accounts in Phoenix, and local business entities—prior to domesticating, ensuring swift pre-judgment or post-domestication attachment.

Judgment Enforcement FAQs

What if the debtor transferred their home or business into a spouse's name?

Transfers to spouses or insiders without fair market consideration are classic 'badges of fraud' under the Uniform Voidable Transactions Act. We document the timing of the transfer relative to the judgment date, establish continuous dominion and control by the debtor, and provide the evidence needed for a court to void the transfer.

Can a private investigator access the debtor's exact bank balance?

Under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA), private investigators cannot obtain real-time bank balances without judicial process or consent. We identify the specific banking institutions, branches, and entity accounts where the debtor deposits funds, allowing your attorney to serve an immediate, enforceable writ of garnishment that freezes all funds present.

How old can a judgment be for recovery?

In Arizona, judgments are valid for 10 years and can be renewed indefinitely under A.R.S. § 12-1612 before expiration. Even if a debtor appeared insolvent five years ago, career promotions, new business formations, inheritance, or real estate acquisitions often create valuable enforcement windows today.
