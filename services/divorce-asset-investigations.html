MATRIMONIAL FINANCIAL FRAUD

Piercing the Veil of Concealed Marital Assets

In high-net-worth divorce litigation—whether in Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, or cross-border multi-state enclaves—financial disclosures sworn under penalty of perjury often obscure substantial portions of the marital estate. When millions of dollars in equity, business ownership, or liquid investments are at stake, standard voluntary discovery frequently fails to reveal the true financial picture.

Under Arizona's community property statutes (A.R.S. § 25-318) and comparable equitable distribution laws nationwide, both spouses have an absolute entitlement to an equitable accounting. When a party intentionally dissipates, diverts, or conceals marital capital, forensic asset tracing is the decisive tool needed to rebalance the scales of justice.

"Wealthy spouses don't simply hide cash in a local safe deposit box; they construct layered labyrinths of pass-through entities, deferred distributions, and out-of-state trusts. Our job is to follow the digital and paper architecture until every dollar is identified."

Six Common Ways Wealthy Spouses Obscure Assets

  • Underreporting Business Revenue & Skimming: Suppressing EBITDA in closely held companies, accelerating discretionary capital expenses, or running personal lifestyle costs through corporate overhead prior to filing.
  • Nominee Shell Entities in Secrecy States: Forming LLCs in Wyoming, Delaware, or Nevada under third-party straw managers to acquire real estate, luxury vehicles, or aircraft away from marital accounts.
  • Collusive Debts & Phantom Creditors: Fabricating non-existent loans or promissory notes payable to cooperative friends or business partners to make thriving estates appear solvent-poor.
  • Deferred Compensation & Hidden Equity Grants: Delaying vesting schedules, stock options, retention bonuses, or partner distributions until after the decree of dissolution is finalized.
  • Offshore & Domestic Asset Protection Trusts (DAPTs): Transferring marital liquid assets into South Dakota, Nevada, or Cook Islands trusts governed by aggressive spendthrift provisions.
  • Cryptocurrency & Digital Cold Storage: Liquidating traditional brokerage accounts to purchase Bitcoin, Ethereum, or privacy coins stored on unhosted hardware wallets.
PROVEN FEDERAL METHODOLOGY

Our Comprehensive 4-Stage Divorce Tracing Protocol

Compitiello & Donofrio Private Investigations approaches matrimonial financial investigations using the rigorous protocols refined across decades of federal anti-money laundering and kleptocracy task force operations:

1. Lifestyle & Expenditure Disparity Audit

We construct a forensic timeline matching declared income on tax filings against verifiable lifestyle expenditures—including private aviation charters, secondary vacation rentals, high-end jewelry purchases, and discretionary credit card spending. Mathematical discrepancies provide judges with prima facie evidence of undisclosed revenue.

2. Entity Deconstruction & Beneficial Ownership Tracing

We systematically audit historical corporate filings, UCC lien registries, real property deeds, commercial loan applications, and digital footprint telemetry to unmask the true beneficial ownership behind complex shell company structures.

3. Multi-Jurisdictional Real Estate & Tangible Asset Mapping

From luxury estates in Paradise Valley and Scottsdale to commercial developments in Texas, California, and Florida, we aggregate property registries, title transfers, and aviation/maritime databases to map all tangible physical holdings.

4. Trial-Ready Forensic Dossiers & Expert Witness Support

We synthesize complex financial findings into clear, incontrovertible court exhibits. Our investigators prepare detailed affidavits, support Rule 49 disclosure demands, and deliver expert testimony under Rule 702 of the Rules of Evidence.

LEGAL & PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Divorce Asset Tracing FAQs

Can a private investigator locate hidden bank accounts legally?

Yes. We strictly comply with federal laws, including the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) and Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). We do not use pretexting to breach bank records. Instead, we identify depository banking relationships, correspondent accounts, and wire transfer trails through legal forensic records, commercial filings, and lifestyle expenditure audits—giving your attorney exact targets for formal subpoenas.

What is marital waste or dissipation, and how do you prove it?

Marital dissipation occurs when one spouse uses marital funds for non-marital purposes during the breakdown of the marriage (e.g., extramarital relationships, undisclosed gambling, transfers to secret paramours, or speculative unauthorized investments). We compile forensic evidence establishing the exact dates, amounts, and beneficiaries of dissipated marital funds to seek dollar-for-dollar restitution.

When should I hire an investigator during the divorce process?

The most effective time to conduct an asset investigation is before filing the petition or early in the mandatory disclosure phase (such as Arizona Rule 49). Pre-filing investigations prevent the concealing spouse from anticipating scrutiny and liquidating or restructuring assets prior to judicial freeze orders.
