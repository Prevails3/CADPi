INTELLIGENCE VS. DATA SCAPING

Why Database Subscriptions Miss Critical Business Risks

Traditional accounting audits and commercial database checks only verify what is already digitized and indexed. Automated tools fail when individuals alter spellings, establish shell companies across state lines, or conceal active litigation in un-syndicated county court dockets.

Compitiello & Donofrio Private Investigations approaches due diligence as an operational intelligence assignment. Spearheaded by former Supervisory Special Agent Michael Compitiello, our team conducts deep human source inquiries, cross-jurisdictional docket searches, and corporate entity deconstruction to protect your capital and brand reputation.

"The greatest risks in corporate mergers are rarely on the balance sheet; they reside in what founders and managing partners deliberately omitted from public view."
INVESTIGATIVE PILLARS

Our Corporate Due Diligence Scope

1. Executive Pedigree & Integrity Verification

Verifying academic credentials, historical corporate directorships, undisclosed bankruptcies, previous regulatory sanctions (SEC, FINRA, FTC), and concealed personal litigation liabilities.

2. Beneficial Ownership & Shadow Partner Tracing

Uncovering ultimate beneficial owners (UBO) hidden behind nominee directors or statutory privacy LLCs to ensure compliance with federal anti-money laundering and OFAC sanctions.

3. Hidden Liens, Factoring & Collateral Encumbrances

Identifying undisclosed merchant cash advances, UCC secondary factoring liens, and contested intellectual property rights that jeopardize investor returns.
