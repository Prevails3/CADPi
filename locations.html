How We Execute Multi-Jurisdictional Investigations

1. Federal Supervisory Direction

Every multi-state case is directly supervised by former federal agents (DHS/HSI / FBI task force leaders), ensuring national-level evidence handling, strict chain of custody, and objective analysis.

2. Reciprocal State Licensing

We operate under direct Arizona DPS licensing (#1616599) and utilize statutory interstate investigator reciprocity and vetted in-state licensed operatives across CA, FL, NY, TX, and beyond.

3. Federal Court & UEFJA Coordination

We compile forensic evidence tailored to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 45, the Uniform Enforcement of Foreign Judgments Act (UEFJA), and local state court disclosure requirements.
